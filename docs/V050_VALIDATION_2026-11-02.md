## Real Arabic audio: paired 10-sample comparison

- Dataset: Bayanat Labs Arabic ASR Channel Pairs v0.1.0,
  `CLEAN/test[0:10]`, derived from Google FLEURS Egyptian Arabic.
- Audio: CC BY 4.0; reference text: CC BY-SA 4.0.
- Each audio asset's SHA-256 was checked against dataset metadata;
  **no audio bytes were redistributed in this repository or artifacts**.
- Reference basis: dataset-published `reference_ar` text, *not*
  an independently human-audited speech transcript. The benchmark
  therefore reports `human_verified_references=0`.
- Comparison: `arabic_basic` normalization and micro-averaged WER/CER.
- Both models: CPU, `int8`, beam size 3, ten identical audio clips.

| Model | Successful clips | Reference words | Micro-WER | Micro-CER |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| faster-whisper `base` | 10/10 | 216 | 0.35185 | 0.11572 |
| faster-whisper `small` | 10/10 | 216 | 0.22685 | 0.06750 |

These are **observations on ten clean read-speech clips**. They do not
establish model superiority on conversational, dialectal, religious or
long-form content. Quantization, model variant, and normalization policy
are part of the result. Compared with base, small improved WER by 0.125
absolute (=12.5 percentage points) on these ten clips.

- Evidence: https://github.com/Mosabomer2000/-nass-mcp/actions/runs/41494878451
- Benchmark implementation: `src/nass_mcp/benchmark.py` and
  `scripts/arabic_asr_benchmark.py`
- Audio manifest producer: `scripts/get_arabic_fixture.py`
- Source: https://huggingface.co/datasets/Bayanat-Labs/arabic-asr-channel-pairs
